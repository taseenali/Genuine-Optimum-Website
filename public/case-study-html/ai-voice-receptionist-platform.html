Check availability
Live calendar look-up that honors business hours and rejects past dates.
List open times
Scans a given day and reads back a short list of real open slots.
Book the appointment
Re-checks availability, guards against duplicates, writes the real event.
Capture the lead
Saves name and callback number the moment they're given.
Flag an emergency
Logs the event and pings the on-call staff member if configured.
Transfer to a human
Hands off directly, or reads back the practice's number.
